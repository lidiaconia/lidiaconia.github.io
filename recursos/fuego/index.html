Nyxia quema un comentario hater

Prompts Nano Banana 2.1 PixVerse · MiniMax ElevenLabs

La imagen, el vídeo y la voz: los 3 prompts para que tu personaje queme el comentario de un hater.

Ver los 3 prompts Ver el resultado ↓

Cómo se hace

  1. Crea el outfit en Google Flow con Nano Banana 2.1, adjuntando una imagen de referencia de tu personaje (prompt 1).
  2. Crea la imagen con el comentario flotando encima, usando la del outfit como referencia (prompt 2).
  3. Sube la imagen a PixVerse, elige el modelo MiniMax en modo Fusion (768P) y pega el prompt del vídeo (prompt 3). La voz de Nyxia está hecha con ElevenLabs.

Truco

  • Cambia el texto del comentario por el comentario más hater que te hayan dejado a ti.

Los 3 prompts

1Outfit 
Full-body photo of the woman from the reference image, same face, same purple hair and same features, wearing a dark glam outfit: a long black patent leather trench coat worn open, a fitted deep purple velvet turtleneck top, a black pleated mini skirt over sheer black tights, knee-high black lace-up boots with silver buckles, a silver choker, long silver earrings and dark plum lipstick, confident queen-like attitude, standing facing the camera, whole body visible from head to feet, plain dark studio background, realistic photo, 9:16 vertical, no text, no logos
2Imagen con el comentario 
Medium shot of the woman from the reference image, same face, purple hair and outfit (open black patent leather trench coat, purple velvet turtleneck, silver choker), standing in a dark moody room with purple and red neon light and light haze, looking at the camera with a calm, unimpressed half smile, holding a silver zippo lighter in one hand. Floating above her head, a large white social media comment card with rounded corners, a small grey round avatar without a face, the username "@hater_anonimo" and the comment text in Spanish: "Esto con IA lo hace cualquiera". The comment card is sharp, readable and slightly glowing. Realistic photo, cinematic lighting, 9:16 vertical, no logos
3Vídeo (PixVerse · MiniMax) 
0-2s: She looks up at the floating hater comment above her head, raises one eyebrow and smirks, and flicks open the silver zippo lighter. She does not speak yet.
2-5s: She sets the comment card on fire from the bottom corner. While it burns, she says with a calm, ironic tone: "¿Que esto lo hace cualquiera?… Pues hazlo tú."
5-6s: The card turns into glowing embers and ash. She blows the ash away toward the camera, silent.
6-10s: She looks straight into the camera with a confident smile and says: "Si quieres saber cómo, escribe FUEGO y te mando el prompt." She winks at the end.
Purple and red neon light, warm flickering firelight on her face, slow push-in camera, realistic fire.

Truco: cambia el texto del comentario por el comentario más hater que te hayan dejado a ti.
