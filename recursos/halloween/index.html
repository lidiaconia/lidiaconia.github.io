01

< Vampira y hombre lobo en la discoteca />

Wan 3.0

Cómo hacerlo

  1. Crea las dos imágenes con estos prompts, adjuntando la hoja de personaje de tu avatar (o una foto tuya) como referencia.
  2. En PixVerse, elige Wan 3.0 en modo Referencia (fusión) y sube el vídeo de movimiento como @video1, la vampira como @image1 y el hombre lobo como @image2.
  3. Pega el prompt del vídeo y genera.

Las imágenes y sus prompts

Prompt del vídeo

Replace the figures in @video1 with those from @image1 and @image2: replace the first person (who is leaning/resting against a railing) with the woman from @image1 — fully replacing their appearance and clothing with her long straight dark purple hair, black leather vampire outfit, long leather coat, glowing ice-blue eyes and fangs — including shots where she is seen from behind or facing away from the camera, where her long purple hair and leather coat must be visible. Replace the second person (the dancer) with the man from @image2 — fully replacing their appearance and clothing with his werewolf look: furry wolf ears, fur on his face and chest, wolf paws with claws, bushy tail, torn black shirt, fur-lined leather jacket and ripped jeans. Retain the background, lighting, camera movement and facial expressions from @video1; the figures from @image1 and @image2 should mimic the actions in @video1 without including the backgrounds from the source images. Keep both faces consistent with @image1 and @image2 throughout. No text, no logos.
02

< El ventilador: cinco disfraces estilo Tim Burton />

Seedance 2.5

Cómo hacerlo

  1. Crea la habitación y los cinco disfraces con estos prompts, adjuntando la hoja de personaje de tu avatar (o una foto tuya) en los disfraces.
  2. En PixVerse, elige Seedance 2.5 y sube el vídeo de movimiento como @video1, la habitación como @image1 y los disfraces como @image2 a @image6, en este orden: rayas, porcelana, bailarina, jardín marchito y circo.
  3. Pega el prompt del vídeo y genera.

El vídeo de referencia solo se usa como guía de movimiento. Usa uno tuyo o uno con permiso.

Las imágenes y sus prompts

Prompt del vídeo

Faithfully follow the motion paths and timing from @video1. Use @video1 only as a motion and timing guide; never copy its person, outfits or room.

Format: 9:16, 1080p, 15 seconds. Fixed 90-degree overhead camera with no camera movement.

Use @image1 as the permanent Gothic room, exactly as shown: purple velvet sofa on the left, marble floor, candles, and the ornate ceiling fan in the foreground at the top of the frame.

FACE LOCK — HIGHEST PRIORITY:
@image2, @image3, @image4, @image5 and @image6 all show the same woman: long straight dark purple hair and black choker. Keep her exact face, face shape, skin tone and body proportions identical in every look. Only the outfit, makeup and accessories change; her facial structure never changes. Keep her face clearly visible in every look. Never use the poses or grey studio backgrounds from the images.

Preserve the opening action from @video1: the woman rolls off the sofa, lands flat on her back, and presses the remote. The ceiling fan remains still before the click and rotates steadily afterward.

After landing, she stays on her back, head toward the top of the frame, feet toward the bottom, legs extended, and full outfit visible.

Timeline:

0–5s — @image2:
The complete black and white striped look: striped corset dress with a spiral skirt and black tulle, striped tights, black buckle boots, tiny top hat with a spiral feather, spiral drawn on her cheek. Reproduce the rolling, falling, landing and remote-control action from @video1.

6–7s — @image3:
A passing fan blade completely hides the transition and reveals the complete broken porcelain doll look: ivory and lilac lace dress with puffed sleeves, big lilac bow, white frilly socks, black Mary Jane shoes, cracked porcelain makeup with rosy cheeks and small heart-shaped red lips. She opens both hands outward and blinks slowly like a doll.

8–10s — @image4:
The next blade reveals the complete music box ballerina look: black velvet bodice, black and purple torn tutu, silver wind-up key on her back, painted doll joints on elbows and knees, black pointe shoes with crossed ribbons, small silver tiara. She lifts both arms gracefully above her head and gives a slow, eerie smile.

11–13s — @image5:
The next blade reveals the complete withered garden queen look: long tattered grey-violet gown wrapped in black thorny vines and dried roses, crown of thorns and dead roses, black lace fingerless gloves, petals scattered on the floor around her. She slowly crosses her hands over her chest and turns her head slightly.

14–15s — @image6:
The final blade reveals the complete moon circus ringmistress look: black and purple striped tailcoat with silver crescent-moon buttons, black corset, black shorts with sheer tights, tall lace-up boots, tall black top hat with a purple band and a small hanging moon, silver star cane. She holds the cane across her body, gives a mischievous smile and taps the hat brim with one finger.

Every transformation must be completely concealed by a passing fan blade. Never blend outfits, makeup or accessories.

Audio: remote click, soft fan sound, and a light whoosh at each transition. No dialogue or background music.

No facial drift, face replacement, mannequin face, hidden face, plastic skin, body deformation, extra limbs, missing accessories, mixed outfits, camera movement, or background changes.
